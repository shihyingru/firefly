# 10 | Governance Charter

> Firefly (螢火) | v0.1 | 2026-07-20 | AGPL-3.0
> This charter is neutrality's verifiable commitment. Any amendment requires a public PR + notice period.

## Mission Boundary

Firefly makes invisible coordinated behavior visible and cultivates users' habits of judgment. Firefly does **not** rule on truth, does **not** comment on stances, and serves **no** political objective.

## Inclusion & Labeling Principles

1. Only coordinated inauthentic behavior (CIB) is labeled: the criterion is behavioral evidence of "accounts acting in concert while pretending to be unrelated," independent of content stance
2. Evidence whitelist: cards present machine-verifiable fields only (doc 04); no stance inference or intent attribution may appear in any system output
3. Coverage discipline: published cases must faithfully reflect the full spectrum detected; selective presentation of one camp is prohibited
4. Source red lines: no non-public government lists, no anonymous tips, no methodology-free partisan lists (doc 09)

## Contributor System

- Anonymity is the default; naming is optional and unlocks only "submit supplementary sources" and "flag clustering errors"
- Spectrum composition target: active arbiters' stance-vector distribution must cover both modes; until met, all cards carry "arbitration spectrum not yet balanced" and the metric is public on the dashboard
- **Badge boundary (hard constraint)**: only profile-page milestone badges are allowed. Public leaderboards, any amplification of "high-tier users' views," and AI-summarized opinion commentary are all prohibited — reputation serves only as internal weight in the bridging engine and must never become a public appeal-to-authority structure
- Popularity display boundary: operational metrics only — "most-queried clusters" and "strong-signal/low-arbitration-coverage clusters"; editorial "importance" judgments are prohibited

## User Data Governance

- The three collection-boundary principles of doc 08 apply: query aggregates allowed; passive reading tracking (even anonymized) prohibited; literacy diary purely on-device, login-free
- Any new data-collection item requires this charter's amendment process (public PR + 30-day notice); nothing may be smuggled in as feature iteration

## Decision Structure

- Initial phase: single maintainer + public decision records. All parameter changes, rule changes, and source additions/removals go through public PRs with a 7-day notice before merge (security hotfixes excepted; disclosed within 72 hours after)
- Growth phase (active arbiters > 1,000): a review panel (3–5 people, openly recruited, cross-spectrum composition required) takes over appeal re-reviews and holds veto power over charter amendments
- Conflict of interest: maintainer and panel members must disclose political-organization positions and funding relationships; recusal on related cases

## Appeals

Per doc 09: 72-hour initial response; panel adjudication; traces kept, no hard deletes; statistics public, identities confidential.

## The Right to Fork (final insurance)

AGPL-3.0 guarantees: if Firefly's governance fails, anyone can take the full code and open data and rebuild. The community's exit right is the most fundamental check on the maintainer.
