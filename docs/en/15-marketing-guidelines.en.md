# 15 | Marketing & Content-Automation Guidelines

> Firefly (螢火) | v0.1 | 2026-07-20 | AGPL-3.0
> Iron rule: all of the automation, none of the disguise. An anti-manipulation tool caught using manipulation tactics loses its credibility irreversibly.

## Account Structure

- One official account per platform (matching @firefly_tw family); bio states "content generated automatically by the system | open source | posting log public"
- The mascot is the spokesperson: the Firefly machine character (3D, produced via the Blender pipeline) — openly non-human, turning transparency into brand identity rather than a disclaimer
- Prohibited: sockpuppet accounts, mutual-boost rings, purchased engagement, any form of astroturfing. This list doubles as our detection criteria for others; crossing it ourselves = self-destruction

## Content Engine (the product is the marketing)

1. **Auto-published cards**: cluster passes arbitration → pipeline auto-renders a shareable card image (timing chart + verifiable fields) and publishes. Disclosure content is itself the strongest material (cf. the organic share performance of DTL's carousel)
2. **Firefly Weekly**: fully auto-generated — weekly cluster count, largest synchronized-posting event, arbitration stats. Data as content; generation rules open source (per doc 12)
3. **Selection discipline**: behavior-evidence cards only; no stance commentary; case coverage faithfully reflects the full spectrum (charter-bound); selection rules public and auditable
4. Posting log fully public: every post annotated "generated by the system at T under rule R"

## The Line on Algorithm Adaptation

- Allowed: studying platform preferences in formats, timing, hashtags (legitimate craft; rules open source)
- Not allowed: coordinated boosting, reciprocal-like networks, engagement-bait misleading titles — whatever we would flag in others is prohibited for us

## Promotion Channels (no ad dependency)

1. **Education path (most label-attack-resistant)**: media-literacy lesson kits — real cluster cases de-identified into teaching material, free for schools/libraries (CC BY)
2. **Inoculation mini-game**: prebunking theory in practice (cf. Cambridge's evidence with the Bad News game) — players role-play the manipulator to learn the tactics; the Firefly mascot guides; spreads independently and funnels to the main product
3. **Newsroom embed widget**: articles citing Firefly cards via embed; every citation = third-party endorsement + exposure
4. **The Threads bot's public replies**: an acquisition channel in themselves (doc 02 bystander conversion)
5. Community collaboration: participate in g0v hackathons etc. as *one channel among many* (talent and feedback), never as an affiliation (doc 10 independence)

## Crisis Communication Rules

- Under labeling attack: produce records only (governance charter, audit log, funding disclosure); no shouting matches, no defensive essays
- System errors (clustering/card mistakes): public correction record within 24 hours; the error itself goes into the weekly report — turning error handling into a transparency showcase
- Consistency check: every marketing action passes one question before publishing — "If someone else did this, would Firefly flag it?" If yes, we don't do it

## Metrics

- North star: query→arbitration conversion rate (reflects trust, not traffic)
- Tracked: per-entry query volume, weekly-report organic shares, lesson-kit downloads, embed citations
- Not chased: follower counts, engagement-rate rankings — volume metrics conflict with arbitration quality (same logic as doc 02's no-leaderboards)
